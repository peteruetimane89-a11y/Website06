Origins · 2009

Foundations in wholesale supply

Was founded in 2009 to offer wholesale electrical material to the commercial, industrial and retail sectors in the Mozambican market. Specializing in LV and MV material with a client base ranging companies, to small and medium size enterprises.

Expansion · 2011

Services joined the core offering

With a continually growing client base Lyzon expanded its offering in 2011 to include electrical construction and maintenance services whilst retaining its core values and offerings.

Complex Projects

Mining, plants, utilities & commercial builds

Since expanded its offering to include electrical construction of more complex projects focusing on commercial and industrial installations, Lyzon gained valuable experience in the field of Mining, Industrial processing plants, Utilities and Commercial installations.

Complementary Services

Full project lifecycle, delivered by qualified teams

Lyzon provides numerous complementary electrical construction services which include field engineering, equipment procurement, material supply, testing and commissioning, and maintenance services, provided by highly qualified and professional teams to cater for the various industry standards and clients requirement.

  • Field Engineering
  • Equipment Procurement
  • Material Supply
  • Testing & Commissioning
  • Maintenance Services: qualified, professional teams
