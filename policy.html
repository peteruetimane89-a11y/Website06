Scope & Management Statement

Commitment to Integrated Management Excellence

Lyzon Engineering & Services, Lda, is a company specialized in the supply of Electrical, Instrumentation, HVAC Automation, Installation and Maintenance in the area of Low and Medium Voltage Electricity, General Mechanics and Solar Photovoltaic System, Civil Construction and Engineering.

Recognizing that quality, health, safety at work and protection of the environment are fundamental pillars for sustainable development and organizational success, Lyzon Engineering & Services, Lda, is committed to implementing, maintaining and continuously improving the integrated management system (IMS) in compliance with the requirements of the standards: ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001:2018 and ISO 14001:2015.

Guiding Beliefs

Beliefs

  • I. All incidents can be prevented and all work can be done safely;

  • II. Managers are responsible for the health and safety of those they control within the work environment and for encouraging the sustainable use of renewable resources;

  • III. It is possible to measure and reduce more and more the environmental impacts of our activities.

  • IV. The quality of our services is the result of commitment, technical competence and continuous improvement of our processes.

Formal Commitments

Commitments

  • (a) To ensure the satisfaction of customers and other stakeholders, meeting the applicable legal, regulatory and contractual requirements;

  • (b) To ensure the safety of our employees and everyone who may be affected by our activities;

  • (c) To train and motivate all employees to consider environmental aspects as an integral and vital element of their daily activities.

Ethics & Integrity Statement

Zero Tolerance for Corruption

A Lyzon Engineering & Services, Lda, undertakes to carry out its activities with ethics, integrity, transparency and responsibility, adopting a policy of zero tolerance regarding any form of corruption, bribery, fraud or illicit practice.

Application

Scope

  • 1. This Policy applies to all employees, administrators, consultants, suppliers, service providers, commercial partners and other third parties who act on behalf of Lyzon Engineering & Services.

Accountabilities

Responsibilities

  • 1. All employees and third parties covered by this Policy are responsible for:

  • 2. Complying with the principles and requirements established in this Policy;

  • 3. Acting with honesty and integrity in the performance of their duties;

  • 4. Reporting any suspicion of corruption, fraud or other illicit conduct through the channels made available by the company.

Binding Commitments

Commitments

(a)

Compliant Practice

To comply with applicable Mozambican legislation and other legal requirements related to the prevention and fight against corruption.

(b)

No Facilitation Payments

To prohibit any form of corruption, bribery or facilitation payment.

(c)

Ethical Culture

To promote a culture of ethics, integrity and transparency in all its activities.

(d)

Safe Reporting

To ensure that relationships with clients, suppliers, partners and public entities are conducted in an integral and impartial manner; providing safe mechanisms for reporting suspected irregularities, guaranteeing confidentiality and protection against retaliation.

(e)

Impartial Investigation

To investigate all complaints impartially and apply the appropriate disciplinary and legal measures whenever non-compliance with this Policy is verified.

Enforcement — Serious Matter

Violations and Sanctions

Failure to comply with this Policy constitutes a serious infringement and may result in the application of disciplinary measures, termination of contracts and other sanctions provided for under applicable legislation.
