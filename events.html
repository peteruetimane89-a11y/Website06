FACIM 2026 LYZON Engineering & Himoinsa Powering Mozambique's Future

FACIM 2026 was an opportunity for LYZON Engineering & Services to strengthen relationships, showcase its capabilities, and engage with clients, industry leaders, business partners, and other stakeholders in Mozambique.

FACIM 2026

Location

Partner

About the Event

Mozambique's flagship industrial gathering

As the country's largest and most comprehensive international trade and industry exhibition, FACIM brings together producers, suppliers, service providers, and decision-makers from every major sector of the Mozambican economy and from every province.

For LYZON Engineering & Services, the 2026 edition was a structured moment to meet long-standing clients, open new technical conversations with project owners and operators, and present the full range of the company's electrical, automation, and maintenance capabilities to a wide national and international audience.

Official Partnership

LYZON × Himoinsa

LYZON participated alongside Himoinsa, its official partner in Mozambique, showcasing power generation solutions alongside LYZON's in-country engineering, project delivery, and aftermarket service capabilities.

The partnership combines Himoinsa's international technology and manufacturing expertise with LYZON's local engineering knowledge, service capabilities, and deep understanding of the Mozambican market. Together this ensures clients receive reliable, engineered, properly supported power generation and electrical solutions from a single accountable team.

International

Himoinsa technology & manufacturing

Local

LYZON engineering & service delivery

Why it matters

A local partner with engineering feet on the ground means shorter lead times, proper installations, correct commissioning, rapid after-sales response, and accountability. All of these are essential for reliable power generation in Mozambique's demanding industrial and commercial operating environments.

What LYZON showcased at FACIM

Across the joint stand, visitors were presented with a clear view of LYZON's end-to-end value proposition. This covers from power generation hardware to commissioned systems and long-term maintenance.

Generator sets and complete power generation systems from the LYZON × Himoinsa portfolio.

Engineering Capabilities

Preventive & corrective maintenance, field support, spares, and long-term service contracts.

Reference Projects

Case studies and completed installations across Mozambique's industrial sectors.

Closing · FACIM 2026

FACIM 2026 demonstrated LYZON Engineering & Services' technical expertise, established local presence, and proven ability to provide reliable electrical and power generation solutions to Mozambique's industrial and commercial sectors. This work is supported by a strong international partner ecosystem.

The event reinforced LYZON's long-standing relationships with customers and partners, and set the basis for new projects across the country.
